Game Description
Minecraft is a sandbox survival adventure game developed by Mojang Studios, part of the globally popular Minecraft franchise. Players explore procedurally generated worlds, craft tools, build structures, and survive against hostile creatures. Offering both single-player and multiplayer modes, it supports extensive customization through skins, mods, and marketplace add-ons, and is available across multiple platforms.
