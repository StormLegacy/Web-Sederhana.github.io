Game Description
Minecraft: Bedrock Edition is the multi-platform version of Minecraft powered by the RenderDragon engine. Built for seamless cross-play, it allows players on consoles, mobile devices, and PCs to play together in the same world. It features smooth performance, official Marketplace content (add-ons, skins, and worlds), built-in parental controls, and native support for touch controls, gamepads, and VR.

  • CPU: Intel Celeron J4105 / AMD FX-4100 (PC) | ARM v8 (Mobile)
  • RAM: 4 GB RAM (PC/Console) | 3 GB RAM (Mobile)
  • GPU: Intel HD Graphics 4000 / NVIDIA GT 730 / AMD Radeon R5 (DirectX 11)
  • Storage: 1 GB (Mobile) | 6 GB+ (PC & Consoles for worlds)
  • Network: Broadband Internet connection for cross-play & Marketplace
